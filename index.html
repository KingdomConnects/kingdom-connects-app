What is Kingdom Connects?

A community-powered directory where church members and faith-aligned business owners connect, grow, and support each other. Discover trusted providers and help small businesses thrive.

Is it free?

Yes. Participation is free. We offer optional Pro features and a way to support the mission, but the core directory will always be free.

Why people use Kingdom Connects

  • Simple submission flow for new businesses
  • Clean, mobile-friendly browsing and quick contact
